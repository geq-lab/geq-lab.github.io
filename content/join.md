# Join us

We take PhD and MTech students through the Discipline of Earth Sciences at IIT Gandhinagar, host postdocs when a project has funding for one, and take a few interns each summer. Formal admission always goes through the institute, and the links below are the right ones. Writing to us first is still a good idea, so that we know you are coming and can tell you whether there is a project for you.

## PhD

Apply through IITGN's [PhD admissions page](https://iitgn.ac.in/admissions/phd) with Earth Sciences as the discipline. Students come to us from geology, geophysics, physics and civil engineering, and what matters is that you can pick up the numerics and the physics as you go. Two students in the group hold fellowships from outside the institute, the Prime Minister's Research Fellowship and the NIDM Doctoral Fellowship. If you are eligible for one of these, or for any other fellowship, say so when you write.

## MTech

The MTech programme in Earth Sciences is a two-year degree with a thesis in the second year. Apply through the [MTech admissions page](https://iitgn.ac.in/admissions/mtech). If you want to do your thesis with us, come and talk to us in your first semester. One of our MTech students stayed on for a PhD with the group, and that route is open.

## Postdocs

Postdoc positions in the group depend on project funding and are advertised on the [IITGN careers page](https://iitgn.ac.in/careers) when one opens. If you have your own fellowship, or are applying for one and need a host, write to Utsav with a CV and a one-page idea.

## Internships

Summer interns come through IITGN's [Summer Research Internship Programme (SRIP)](https://srip.iitgn.ac.in/), which runs over May to July. Students registered at another institute who want a longer research stay can apply through the [Visiting Students Programme](https://iitgn.ac.in/admissions/visiting-students-programme). Recent interns have worked on probabilistic tsunami hazard, lineament detection from elevation models with neural networks, phase picking with PhaseNet, and the group website.

## What we look for

Most of what we do is computational, so you should be comfortable with mathematics and willing to write code, in Python, MATLAB or C. The rest is curiosity about how the Earth works and the patience to find out why a model or a dataset does what it does. You do not need to have done geophysics before.

## How to write to us

Email Utsav Mannu at utsav.mannu@iitgn.ac.in. Put in your CV, your marks so far, and a short note on which of the [research themes](/research/) interests you and why. A few lines on what you have actually done, a project, a thesis, a piece of code, are worth more than a long statement of purpose. Do read the admissions pages first; the formal application goes through them, not through us.
