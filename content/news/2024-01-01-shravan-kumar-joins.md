Dr Shravan Kumar joins the group as a postdoctoral fellow. He has a PhD in geophysics from Banaras Hindu University on the crustal structure of the Arabian Basin, and works here on marine geophysics and forward gravity modelling, and on thermomechanical models of rift inversion in the Cambay basin.
