Shikha Sharma received her PhD at IIT Gandhinagar's 15th Convocation on 20 June 2026. Her thesis, "Development of regionally adaptable Ground Motion Prediction Equation (GMPE) for the Himalayan region", was assessed as Outstanding by the final defence committee. She was co-supervised by Sanjay Singh Bora (Earth Sciences New Zealand).
