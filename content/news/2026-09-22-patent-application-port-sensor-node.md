title: "Patent application on a sensor node for port structures"
date: 2026-09-22
people: [utsav-mannu]
---
IIT Gandhinagar has filed an Indian patent application (202621113469, 22 September 2026) for a TinyML-enabled wireless sensor node that classifies the structural health of marine port infrastructure on the node itself. The inventors are Sandeep Gupta, Srinivasan Chandrasekaran and Utsav Mannu. Sandeep's PhD in Maritime Engineering is supervised jointly by Prof. Chandrasekaran and Utsav.
