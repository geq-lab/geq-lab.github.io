Three abstracts from the group were accepted for the EGU General Assembly 2026 in Vienna (3 to 8 May 2026). Gulshan Kumar went to Vienna with "High-pressure elastic properties of GeO2 polymorphs up to 120 GPa" (Gulshan Kumar, Sumit Ghosh, Sharad Babu Pillai and Rajkrishna Dutta), in session GMPV8.2, with travel support from IITGN. Omkar's poster "Comparison and reliability of declustering methods evaluated using an ETAS framework" (with Shikha Sharma, Shyam Nandan and Utsav Mannu) was scheduled in session SM8.1 on 5 May 2026, and Shikha's "A Uniformly Processed Strong-Motion Flat-File for Crustal Earthquakes across the Indian Region" (with Omkar, Utsav Mannu and Sanjay Singh Bora) in session SM3.3 on 6 May 2026.
