Two abstracts from the group were accepted for the EGU General Assembly 2026 in Vienna. Omkar's poster "Comparison and reliability of declustering methods evaluated using an ETAS framework" (with Shikha Sharma, Shyam Nandan and Utsav Mannu) was scheduled in session SM8.1 on 5 May 2026, and Shikha's "A Uniformly Processed Strong-Motion Flat-File for Crustal Earthquakes across the Indian Region" (with Omkar, Utsav Mannu and Sanjay Singh Bora) in session SM3.3.
