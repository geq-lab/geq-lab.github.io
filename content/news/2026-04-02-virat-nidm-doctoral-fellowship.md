Virat Upadhyay has been awarded the National Institute of Disaster Management's Doctoral Fellowship for 2025-26 for his proposal "Toward Integrated Probabilistic Hazard Assessment: Integrating Multivariate Geological Factors and Surface Topography". Dr Pankaj Kumar of NIDM joins as his co-supervisor.
