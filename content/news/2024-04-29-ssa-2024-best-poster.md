title: "Shikha wins an SSA Student Presentation Award"
date: 2024-04-29
people: [shikha-sharma, omkar, utsav-mannu]

Shikha Sharma received a 2024 Annual Meeting Student Presentation Award of the Seismological Society of America for her poster at the SSA Annual Meeting in Anchorage (29 April to 3 May 2024), "Developing a hybrid ground-motion modeling framework for the Himalayan region of India" (Shikha Sharma, Utsav Mannu and Sanjay Singh Bora). Omkar presented a second poster, "Extended finite-fault ground motion modeling framework: sensitivity analysis of number of sub-faults" (Omkar, Shikha Sharma, Utsav Mannu and Sanjay Singh Bora).
