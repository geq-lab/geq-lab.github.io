---
title: "Shikha wins a best poster award at SSA 2024"
date: 2024-04-29
people: [shikha-sharma, omkar, utsav-mannu]
---
Shikha Sharma won a Best Poster Award at the Seismological Society of America Annual Meeting 2024 in Anchorage (29 April to 3 May 2024) for "Developing a hybrid ground-motion modeling framework for the Himalayan region of India" (Shikha Sharma, Utsav Mannu and Sanjay Singh Bora). Omkar presented a second poster, "Extended finite-fault ground motion modeling framework: sensitivity analysis of number of sub-faults" (Omkar, Shikha Sharma, Utsav Mannu and Sanjay Singh Bora).
