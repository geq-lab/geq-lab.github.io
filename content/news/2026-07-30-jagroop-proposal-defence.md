Jagroop Singh defended his PhD proposal, "Significance of oblique subduction in initiation and evolution of back-arc rifting: A 3D Geodynamic Modeling Study", on 30 July 2026. He builds 3D thermomechanical models of oblique subduction and compares them with the Andaman-Sumatra region.
