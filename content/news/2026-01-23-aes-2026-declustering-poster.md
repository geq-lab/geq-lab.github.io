We took six contributions to the International Conference on Intraplate and Himalayan Seismology and the 9th Annual Convention on Advances in Earthquake Science (AES-2026) at the Institute of Seismological Research, Gandhinagar, from 23 to 25 January 2026. Utsav gave a talk on geodynamic models of subduction wedges and strain accumulation due to the smectite to illite transformation. Omkar, Dakshata Bhamare, Sumit Singh, Virat Upadhyay and Shikha Sharma presented posters, on earthquake declustering and the Gutenberg-Richter b value, ETAS models of megathrust earthquakes, intraplate earthquakes, surface curvature in earthquake amplification and landslide hazards, and Himalayan ground-motion models tested against global active-crust models in OpenQuake. The co-authors are listed with the abstracts on the Publications page. Shikha's poster took third prize in the poster competition.
