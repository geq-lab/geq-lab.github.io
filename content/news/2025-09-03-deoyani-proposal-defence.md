Deoyani Shelke defended her PhD proposal, on the phase stability of (Pb,Zn)S solid solutions from density functional theory, on 3 September 2025. She now works on the high-pressure behaviour of cobalt phosphide (Co2P), combining density functional theory with experiments done with collaborators at Kumamoto University.
