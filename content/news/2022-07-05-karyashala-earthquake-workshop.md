We ran a SERB Accelerate Vigyan Karyashala, a workshop and hands-on training in "Emerging Approaches in Earthquake Occurrence and Risks", at IITGN from 5 to 12 July 2022. Utsav organised it with Ramendra Sahoo. The speakers were Masataka Kinoshita (ERI, University of Tokyo), Sanjay Bora (GNS Science), Shyam Nandan (ETH Zurich), R. N. Singh (IITGN), Yavor Kamer (RichterX) and Aitaro Kato (University of Tokyo).
