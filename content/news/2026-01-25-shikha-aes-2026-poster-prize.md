Shikha Sharma's poster "Evaluation of Himalayan Ground Motion Models with Global Active Shallow Crustal Models Using OpenQuake" (Shikha Sharma, Omkar, Sanjay Singh Bora, Shyam Nandan and Utsav Mannu) took third prize in the poster competition at AES-2026 at the Institute of Seismological Research, Gandhinagar, on 25 January 2026.
