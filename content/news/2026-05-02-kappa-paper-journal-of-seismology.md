"Seismic attenuation in North-East India: estimation and analysis of the kappa parameter" is out in Journal of Seismology (volume 30, article 42). Lalramnunkimi, who worked with us on her Master's dissertation in 2024, is first author, with Shikha Sharma, Lalruatpuia Tlau, Zosangliana Ralte and Saitluanga Sailo of Pachhunga University College and Mizoram University, Aizawl, and Utsav Mannu. The waveform dataset behind the paper is on Figshare (doi 10.6084/m9.figshare.31876414).
