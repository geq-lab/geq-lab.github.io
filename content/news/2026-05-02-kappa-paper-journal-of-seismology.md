"Seismic attenuation in North-East India: estimation and analysis of the kappa parameter" is out in Journal of Seismology (volume 30, article 42). Lalramnunkimi, who worked with us on her Master's thesis in 2024, is first author, with Shikha Sharma, L. Tlau, Z. Ralte, S. Sailo and Utsav Mannu. The waveform dataset behind the paper is on Figshare (doi 10.6084/m9.figshare.31876414).
