Shikha Sharma defended her PhD thesis, "Development of regionally adaptable Ground Motion Prediction Equation (GMPE) for the Himalayan region", on 22 December 2025. Prof. S. T. G. Raghukanth (IIT Madras) was the external examiner. Shikha joined the department in 2019 and the group in 2021, and was co-supervised by Sanjay Singh Bora.
