Sanjay Singh Bora of GNS Science, New Zealand, who co-supervises Shikha Sharma and works with Omkar on ground-motion simulation, visited IIT Gandhinagar from 22 to 26 February 2025. On 25 February he gave the fifth talk in the Earth Science Lecture Series, "New Zealand National Seismic Hazard Model Revision 2022: key aspects and changes to NZ NSHM 2010".
