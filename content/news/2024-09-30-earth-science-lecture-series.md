Utsav started the department's Earth Science Lecture Series with a talk by Dr Shailesh Nayak, Director of the National Institute of Advanced Studies, Bengaluru, "Climate Change: Implications and Way Forward", on 30 September 2024, with Virat Upadhyay helping to run it. The series has since hosted ten more speakers, and colleagues in the department now run it.
