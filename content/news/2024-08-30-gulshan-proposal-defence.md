Gulshan Kumar defended his PhD proposal, "High-pressure studies of minerals", on 30 August 2024. The talk was on ZnS, PbS and their solid solution. He now works on the rutile to CaCl2-type transition in GeO2, an analogue for SiO2, with density functional theory.
