Shikha Sharma and Omkar presented at 18WCEE in Milan, 30 June to 5 July 2024. Shikha's paper, "Evaluation of ground-motion models for Himalayan region" (with Utsav Mannu and Sanjay Singh Bora), and Omkar's paper on optimising seismological parameters in extended finite-fault simulation with GMSS2.0 are both in the open-access proceedings.
