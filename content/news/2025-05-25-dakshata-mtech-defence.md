Dakshata Bhamare defended her MTech thesis, "Geological Insights into Megathrust Earthquake Dynamics through ETAS Modeling", on 25 May 2025. She fitted the epidemic-type aftershock sequence model to earthquakes of magnitude 4 and above from 1960 to 2024 in the Andaman, Alaska-Aleutian and Tonga subduction zones and compared the ETAS parameters with plate age, trench sediment thickness, seafloor roughness and plate velocities. R. N. Singh and Projesh Nath Choudhury examined the thesis with Utsav.
